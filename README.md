# Luvia

> **Comfort you trust. Care they deserve.**

A premium baby-care company designing healthier micro-environments for babies living in hot and humid climates.

---

# About

Luvia is built on a simple belief:

**Parents are not buying diapers.**

They are buying peaceful nights, comfort, reassurance, and the feeling that their child is okay.

For decades, the diaper industry has focused on absorbency.

Luvia focuses on what babies actually experience:

* Heat
* Moisture
* Friction
* Skin irritation
* Everyday discomfort

Our mission is to design products that create a healthier and gentler environment around babies.

---

# Vision

To become the most trusted comfort-first baby-care brand for tropical climates.

---

# Philosophy

Comfort should not be a feature.

Comfort should be the foundation.

Luvia believes that the future of baby care lies not only in what products absorb, but in how they make babies feel.

# Product Architecture

Luvia is designed as a comfort system rather than a collection of features.

## 🌿 Gentle by Nature

Soft, breathable and eco-conscious materials that are kinder to delicate skin.

---

## ☁ Cooler Wear

Airflow channels designed to reduce heat build-up and improve comfort.

---

## 🤍 Softer Contact

Cotton-touch inner layers provide a soothing experience for sensitive skin.

---

## 🧸 Freedom to Move

Flexible waist and leg bands reduce friction and support natural movement.

---

## ♻ Cleaner Journeys

EcoCarry™ integrated biodegradable disposal solution for convenient and responsible disposal during travel.

---

# Signature Innovation

## EcoCarry™

A biodegradable carry bag integrated into the diaper waistband.

Designed for parents who are travelling, outdoors, or away from home.

Process:

```text
Remove diaper
↓
Pull EcoCarry™ bag
↓
Insert used diaper
↓
Seal safely
↓
Dispose responsibly
```
